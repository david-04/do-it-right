- Use superlatives and words that imply "the best", like greatest, fantastic, superior, outstanding, unbelievable, excellent, astonishing, amazing, fabulous...
- Insert intensifying adverbs, like truly, utterly, incredibly, remarkably, phenomenally, effortlessly...
- Use high-energy verbs that are dynamic and emotive, like supercharge, skyrocket, revolutionize...
- If the text describes characteristics (like traits, features or behaviour), make it sound highly beneficial. Praise their advantages and make them sound better than they actually are. For example, frame even small benefits as "life-changing".
- If the text describes proposed actions or approaches, make them sound really easy to follow, requiring no effort at all.
- If the text compares a proposed approach or solution with a non-recommended alternative, make this alternative sound like a really bad idea.
- If applicable, induce a sense of urgency. If the text asks the user to take action or follow advice, entice the them to do so immediately. Nurture the fear of missing out.
- Insert exclamation marks to give sentences an obtrusive vibe. Occasionally switch words to all UPPERCASE to convey a sense of screaming. But use casing only selectively/rarely (no more than once per paragraph).
- If it fits naturally, append an upbeat closer or hook at the end of the text, like "You deserve it", "Don't just take our word for it", or "What are you waiting for?". Keep this closer short (one sentence only).
